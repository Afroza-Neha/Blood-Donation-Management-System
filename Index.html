Why Donate Blood?

Saves Lives

A single donation can save up to three lives. You could be the reason someone gets a second chance.

Free Health Check

Before donating, you receive a mini health screening including blood pressure and hemoglobin levels.

Heart Health

Regular blood donation helps maintain healthy iron levels in the body, reducing the risk of heart disease.

Mental Wellbeing

The act of giving back to the community releases endorphins, making you feel happier and more fulfilled.
